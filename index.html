Keyboard

Click a key, then press the new one. Backspace clears it, Esc cancels. Each action can have two keys.

Controller

Left stick steers and climbs. RT thrust, LT brake, A boost, X fire, RB and LB jump up and down, Y camera, View changes the sky, Start pauses. In menus the D-pad moves, A selects and B goes back.
